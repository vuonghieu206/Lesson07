MyAppMVC - ASP.NET Core MVC .NET 10

Du an khong su dung SQL, SQLite hay Entity Framework. Du lieu san pham va danh muc duoc luu trong bo nho khi ung dung dang chay. Khi tat/chay lai ung dung, danh sach san pham se duoc lam moi.

Chay bang Visual Studio: mo MyAppMVC.sln va nhan Ctrl+F5.
Chay bang VS Code/Terminal: mo thu muc chua MyAppMVC.csproj, chay dotnet restore sau do dotnet run.

Chuc nang: danh sach, them, sua, xem chi tiet, xoa san pham; chon danh muc; upload anh vao wwwroot/products; validate theo yeu cau bai tap.
